# A Pickup starts, or upgrades the bench

A Pickup that the Lineup would start comes first, under the starter it would replace. To find one, the panel builds the Lineup again with the available player added. The other Pickups rank above the user's lowest-ranked bench player in the same list. They are bench upgrades, and the panel shows them only on request.

## Why

Before, a Pickup ranked above the user's lowest-ranked active player. That player is often a handcuff at #139, so almost every free player from #1 to #138 was a Pickup, and a defense swap was at the bottom of the list.

The other way was to compare ranks with the user's lowest-ranked starter in the same list. A FLEX list can rank a free TE above the user's RB2, but the TE cannot play RB, and he does not start if he is below the FLEX starter. To build the Lineup again uses the same rules as the Lineup, so the two cannot disagree. It also names the correct starter: a free RB who takes the RB Slot moves the RB to FLEX, and the WR at FLEX goes to the bench.

The cost is one Lineup for each available player that a list ranks. To check only the ones ranked above a starter would miss a Slot that changes lists: when its widest list (a short preview) ranks none of the user's players, the Slot reads the next one, and it reads the preview again once it ranks the added player. With five lists of 400 players, the Pickups take 5 ms to find.
