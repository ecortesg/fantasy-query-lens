# The panel follows the tab on scanned sites

The panel shows the Lineup of the tab in front. On a site that has kept lists and that the user allowed, the panel reads each page the tab shows: when the tab comes to the front, when it loads, and when the page draws new content over the marks, such as a tab click. A read marks the players and shows the Lineup kept for the page's site and week. The Source card says "Ranks out of date" when the page shows a kept list that now ranks a player differently. A read keeps no list: only a Scan does (ADR-0007). On other pages, such as Sleeper, the panel keeps the last Lineup it showed. When the user removes the last list of a Source, its marks go and the panel says "No scan yet". A page that draws its table after it loads is watched for 10 seconds for the players.

## Why

The Lineup was tied to one Scan of one tab. A reload, another page of the same site, or a closed panel lost the highlights, and "page changed" notes asked for a Rescan with nothing new to read. Users think of a rankings site and week, not of a Scan: Fantasy Footballers has one page per position, and once all five are scanned the user expects each one to show their players. A read keeps no list, so the problems of ADR-0009, lists that come and go and ranks in the wrong format, do not come back. After a reload Chrome lets a script in only with site access, so with a toolbar click alone the marks wait for the next click. Yahoo can serve a first page with older ranks than its tabs load; "Ranks out of date" then shows, which is true of what the page shows.
